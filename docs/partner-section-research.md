# Partner section · research

Written 2026-10-19 for the site v5 partner section (branch redesign/site-v5-partners). Phase 1 of the owner's brief: what the programmes allow, how other firms present partnerships, how to announce ours, and how a partner section fits the v5 design. Every claim about the programmes cites a primary source; where only the partner portal has the answer, it is listed as a question at the end.

## 1. Official rules

### 1.1 Anthropic: the Claude Partner Network

**Name.** The programme is the **Claude Partner Network**, run by Anthropic. It launched on 12 March 2026 ("Anthropic invests $100 million into the Claude Partner Network", https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-partner-network). The old site's "Anthropic Partner Network · Phase 1" is not the programme's name and should not be reused.

**Tiers.** On 3 June 2026 Anthropic added the **Services Track** and the **Partner Hub** (https://www.anthropic.com/news/services-track-partner-hub). Quoted from that page:

- "new applicants start at Registered, the program's entry level, with a minimum commitment to 10 certified practitioners."
- "Partnership begins at Select."
- Select: at least 10 active certified individuals, at least 2 joint customers deployed in production in the trailing 12 months, at least 1 public customer story.
- Preferred: at least 100 certified individuals, 15 deployed joint customers, 3 public stories.
- Global Premier: at least 1,000 certified individuals, 100 deployed joint customers across three or more regions, 15 public stories, a joint business plan with named executive sponsors.
- "we verify standing every quarter."

**Approved term.** This is the most important finding. Anthropic's own wording reserves "partner" for **Select and above**: "Partnership begins at Select." The March announcement calls joining "membership" ("Membership is free of charge"). So:

- At **Select or higher**: "partner" is the programme's own word ("Select partner in the Claude Partner Network").
- At **Registered**: Anthropic does not say what a Registered firm may call itself. "Partner" would contradict "Partnership begins at Select"; "member of the Claude Partner Network" matches Anthropic's March wording but is not confirmed for Registered firms. Treat this as a question for the portal's co-marketing guidelines, not an assumption.

A three-person firm cannot yet have 10 active certified practitioners, which Select requires, so Solven is most likely at Registered. Only the Partner Hub dashboard can confirm it.

**Directory (verification).** https://claude.com/partners links "Find a partner" to the Services Partner Directory, which redirects to https://partnerhub.claude.com/directory. Each firm has a profile at `https://partnerhub.claude.com/directory/partner/<id>`, showing a "Partner Tier" badge (Select, Preferred or Global Premier), a description, locations, industries and services. Example: https://partnerhub.claude.com/directory/partner/sqe-partner (SQE, Jakarta, tier "Select"). The tier filter offers only Select, Preferred and Global Premier, so Registered firms are probably not listed; the announcement says only that "every partner's standing" is visible there. Whether Solven has a profile is a question.

**Logo and brand rules.** Anthropic publishes no partner badge or logo kit. Its Trademark Guidelines (effective 1 August 2024, https://www.anthropic.com/legal/trademark-guidelines) say, quoted:

- "You may only use our trademarks as specifically permitted by us and only in materials we approve beforehand."
- "No alterations of our trademarks (changes to color, font, proportion, or otherwise) are permitted."
- "You may not use our trademarks in a manner that implies Anthropic's sponsorship or endorsement" except as expressly authorised.
- "Refrain from placing the trademarks on any background that interferes with the trademarks' readability or display."
- "Maintain a reasonable space between each side of our trademark and other graphic or textual elements."
- "Do not use a trademark symbol with the trademarks."
- "If you have an existing business relationship with Anthropic and want to use our trademarks in a press release [or on a website], please email marketing@anthropic.com."

"Trademarks" there include "our names, logos, brand elements". The partner page says the Partner Portal holds "co-marketing guidelines" (https://claude.com/partners, login at https://partnerhub.anthropic.com/). I cannot see the portal, so the rules for partner badges, website use and announcements are a question.

**Announcing.** Nothing public beyond the trademark rule above: website and press use of the marks goes through marketing@anthropic.com unless the portal's co-marketing guidelines grant it already.

### 1.2 OpenAI: the OpenAI Partner Network

openai.com refuses automated requests (HTTP 403), so the pages below were read from Internet Archive captures of openai.com; each is cited by its live URL with the capture date.

**Name.** The **OpenAI Partner Network**, announced 14 June 2026 ("Introducing the OpenAI Partner Network", https://openai.com/index/introducing-openai-partner-network/, capture of 2026): "a new program for partners around the world to build, sell, and deliver AI solutions with OpenAI." It replaces the earlier Services Partner Program, which some firms announced in 2025 (for example Reply, https://www.reply.com/en/newsroom/news/reply-announces-collaboration-with-openai-within-the-services-partner-program).

**Tiers.** "Partners can progress through three tiers: Select, Advanced, and Elite, each with a high bar for sales performance, technical capability, co-sell engagement, and deployment experience." Specialisations are planned: "Codex, cybersecurity, and agents". The thresholds are not public.

**Approved term.** OpenAI calls programme members "partners" throughout. The partners page (https://openai.com/business/partners/, capture of 12 October 2026) describes the first step as "Enroll: Share your company's capabilities, technical expertise, and customer experience to be considered for the OpenAI Partner Network." Enrolment is therefore an application. Whether Solven's acceptance gives it a tier (Select) or only portal access is a question.

OpenAI's brand guidelines are explicit about the word: "OpenAI has official partnerships and specific language about these partnerships, as well as non-partnership language guidance. If you are not an official partner, please don't use 'collaborated with,' 'worked with,' or 'partnered with,' in any form." (https://openai.com/brand/, capture of 1 January 2026; the October 2026 capture is blocked.) So "partner" is fine once Solven is an official partner, and must not be used before.

**Directory (verification).** The partners page links "Find a partner: Access our partner locator" to https://openai.com/business/partners/locator/. The locator loads by script, so I could not see whether firms have individual profile URLs or tier badges. Whether Solven is listed is a question.

**Logo and brand rules** (https://openai.com/brand/, capture of 1 January 2026), quoted:

- "Use the logo only when it directly relates to OpenAI services." "Use the logo exactly as provided and acknowledge that it belongs to OpenAI."
- Don't "Use the logo without permission or outside OpenAI's terms." Don't "Misrepresent your relationship with OpenAI, imply endorsement, or confuse users about sponsorship." Don't "Use the logo more prominently than your own or in unrelated contexts."
- Brand partnerships: "maintain balanced spacing and clear hierarchy. Avoid placing logos too close to one another… Ensure each logo is displayed at an appropriate size, uses its approved color format, and is not distorted or altered in any way."
- "All co-branded materials must undergo an approval process by both brands."
- Partnership lockup: don't "add any colors", don't use "the wordmark at very different sizes", don't use "the Blossom with the partnership lock-up", don't place it "over a busy image"; do "strive to have the wordmarks be the same height".
- The primary wordmark "requires prescribed clear space and should NOT be used with the Blossom."
- Usage terms: "Do not feature our Marks more prominently than your own company's name or marks." "We can review the use of our Marks and require changes if needed."

**Announcing.** Same page: "When mentioning our company in press materials, please identify OpenAI as an AI research and deployment company." Anything beyond the published cases goes to OpenAI's Partner Communications team, partnercomms@openai.com.

### 1.3 What this means for Solven, in short

1. The claim must match the tier. "Partner" is safe for Anthropic only at Select or higher, and for OpenAI only once Solven is an official partner (not just enrolled). The current v5 line, "Partner of Anthropic and OpenAI", may overclaim on the Anthropic side.
2. Name the programmes, not the companies: "the Claude Partner Network" and "the OpenAI Partner Network". "Partner of Anthropic" reads as a bilateral deal; programme names are what the directories verify.
3. Logos need permission. Anthropic requires prior approval of every use; OpenAI requires approval of co-branded material. A text-only version needs no artwork approval, though Anthropic counts its name as a trademark too, so the safest course is to send the final wording to both partner teams before launch.
4. Never: "endorsed by", "certified by Anthropic/OpenAI" (unless the certification is a named exam the team holds), "official partner", "strategic partner", "preferred partner" (Preferred is a real Anthropic tier), "approved", "Phase 1", or a ™ symbol.

### 2.1 Patterns

1. **The exact programme name and tier, plus one proof, reads as credible** ("Preferred Partner in the Claude Partner Network", "OpenAI Advanced Partner backed by 400+ AI coaches"). A bare "partner" or "we work with the labs" reads as decoration.
2. **Almost nobody links to the official listing.** None of the fifteen linked a partnerhub.claude.com profile, the OpenAI locator, AWS Partner Finder or a Microsoft profile. A real verification link would set Solven apart, but only if Solven is listed: the Anthropic directory filters only Select, Preferred and Global Premier.
3. **Finance-grade firms keep it quiet.** Sia, Accordion and Aventis keep partnerships out of the hero: a dated release, one text line, or capability first. Logo walls (V2, Quandary, Simpson) read as technology vendors.
4. **One wording everywhere.** Vantage Point's "Official Claude Partner" against "Member" is the clearest trust leak in the sample.
5. **Say what it means for the client** (Caylent: "move Claude into production, faster"; 10Clouds: what the tier certifies).
6. **Two labs, equal weight** (Artium, Vantage Point). For Solven this carries a message of its own: independence from either vendor.
7. **Lab-authored evidence beats self-description**: a link to the lab's directory or announcement, or a quote from the lab.
8. **Restraint about tier.** Without an earned tier, say "member" or say nothing about tier. "Official" or "certified" without a tier invites doubt now that the directories print tiers.

## 3. Announcing it

**On the site.** The section itself (below), with the same wording on every page that mentions it. No press release page: a news item would be the only one on the site and would date quickly.

**LinkedIn.** One company-page post, reshared by the three co-founders, after both partner teams have approved the wording (Anthropic: marketing@anthropic.com unless the portal's co-marketing guidelines already cover it; OpenAI: partnercomms@openai.com for anything beyond its published cases). Short, in the house voice, built on the client benefit and the independence angle, not on the badge. Suggested shape, three sentences:

> Solven Advisory has joined the Claude Partner Network and the OpenAI Partner Network. For the private equity firms we work with, it means training and support from both model makers, and advice that is not tied to either. We will keep using whichever model fits the work.

(The first sentence changes to "is a Select partner in…" if that is the tier. No lab logos in the post image unless the portals supply partner badges for this use; a plain typographic card in the site's style is safer. If mentioning OpenAI in a press-style text, describe it as "an AI research and deployment company", per its brand page.)

**An insight article: yes, one.** A short note in the Insights series, for example "Why we work with both model makers", is a better fit for a boutique with a point of view than a press release. It turns the credential into the thesis (judgment first, the model second, the right model for the task) and gives the LinkedIn post something to link to. It must stay within the programme facts in section 1 and must not quote either lab without permission.

**A news line: no.** The site has no news section, and one dated item would age badly.

## 4. Fit with the v5 design

Sources read: docs/design-brief.md (sections 1, 4, 5.1, 6.4, 8, 11), explorations/site-v5/site-v5.css, explorations/site/site.css, tools/build_site_v5.py and tools/build_site_v4.py (close(), homepage()). Homepage screenshot at 1440: calçada hero (ground #FAF9F6 with the pattern at 12%), then the beige services band (#EBE9E1) with the four azulejo cards, the dark convictions band (#2A2A28), the insights on the ground, the closing on the band with the MAAT and the partner line in small text, the dark footer.

- **There is already a reserved slot.** The brief's Direction 1 layout (9.1, row 3) puts a ruled facts strip straight after the hero, 112px tall, with "Anthropic Partner Network" as one of its four cells (6.4). The partner section can take that slot and that height, so the homepage stays close to its 3,000px budget (it is 3,214px now; the strip adds about 120 to 160px).
- **Surface.** Plain ground (#FAF9F6), 1px rules top and bottom in --c-rule, no box, no shadow, no radius. It separates the textured hero from the beige band, so it reads as a hinge rather than a new block. Text: 18px sans in --c-text, 14px --c-muted for the caption (5.2:1 on the ground, AA).
- **Logos as a credential, not a logo wall.** Only two marks, side by side at the same height (OpenAI's lockup rule), each with its programme name in text beside or below it, each a link to the lab's own listing where one exists. No cards, no tiles, no carousel, no hover effects beyond a text underline. The sentence carries the claim; the marks only confirm it.
- **Colour conflict to resolve.** Both labs forbid recolouring. Official files are black or full colour (Anthropic's mark in its terracotta; any Claude Partner Network badge would bring cream and terracotta and an uppercase tracked "MEMBER", which our own rules ban for our type but which we may not alter in theirs). Black #000 is not our ink, and terracotta is not in our palette. Options: use each lab's approved one-colour black version at small size, which reads as neutral against our near-black text; or keep the section text-only. The text-only version needs no artwork permission and fits the "type and colour" character of the brief best.
- **What it must not become.** Not an icon-per-feature card row, not an eyebrow label ("PARTNERS" in tracked capitals), not numbered, not animated, not on a dark band with glows.

## 5. Recommendation

**Placement: directly after the hero, before the services.** The owner's instinct is right, for four reasons. (1) Trust before the pitch: a PE reader asks "who are these people" before "what do they sell", and with no client logos or case numbers allowed, this is the strongest third-party fact the site can show. (2) It is the slot the brief already reserved for exactly this (6.4, 9.1), at a known height. (3) Not inside the hero: two lab marks next to the H1 would compete with our own name and break OpenAI's rule against featuring its marks more prominently than ours. (4) The finance-grade pattern (section 2.1, point 3) is restraint: a thin ruled line, not a banner.

**Form: a ruled credential line.** One sentence naming both programmes, one short clause on what it means for clients, the two marks at equal height (only with permission; otherwise text), each linked to its verification listing (only where one exists). About 120px at 1440, stacking on phones.

**Wording (pick one; the tier words depend on the answers in section 6):**

1. If Solven is at **Select or higher** in both: "Solven is a Select partner in the Claude Partner Network and the OpenAI Partner Network. We train with both model makers and advise independently of either."
2. If Solven is **Registered / enrolled, not yet tiered**: "Solven is a member of the Claude Partner Network and the OpenAI Partner Network. We work with both model makers, and recommend whichever fits the task." ("Member" is Anthropic's own word in the March announcement; it is the safe term below Select, subject to the portal's guidance.)
3. Shorter, capability first (works at any tier): "Members of the Anthropic and OpenAI partner programmes, and independent of both: the model is chosen for the work, never the other way round." (Uses the company names with "programmes", which is accurate at any level but less checkable than the programme names.)

**Verification links.** Link each mark or programme name to Solven's own listing (partnerhub.claude.com/directory/partner/<id>; the OpenAI locator entry) if they exist. If not, link nothing rather than linking the programme's home page, which proves nothing about Solven.

**The closing line.** Once the strip exists, drop the partner line from the homepage closing (one mention per page). Keep it on the other pages, where the strip does not appear, in the same wording as the strip.

**Structured data.** Add memberOf to the ProfessionalService in HOME_LD, as two ProgramMembership entries (programName "Claude Partner Network", hostingOrganization Anthropic; programName "OpenAI Partner Network", hostingOrganization OpenAI), with no tier unless the tier is confirmed. That states membership without implying endorsement. Remove "Anthropic Claude" and "OpenAI ChatGPT" from knowsAbout only if the owner wants; they are not a claim.

### Alternatives

- **A. Closing only.** Keep the partner mention in the closing band, upgraded from plain text to the two marks and the verification links. No new section and no extra height, but it sits at the bottom where skimmers rarely arrive.
- **B. Hero subline.** One short line in the hero side column under the lead, text only ("Member of the Claude and OpenAI partner networks"). Zero extra height and top visibility, but it crowds the hero and cannot carry marks or links comfortably.
